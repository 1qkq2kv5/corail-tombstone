## 📖 About

🔹 **Corail Tombstone** is a powerful tool built to help you recover your Minecraft items after death with an enchanted tombstone that safely stores your inventory. Corail Tombstone spawns a grave at your death location, keeping items safe until you return to retrieve them.

🔸 Whether you're a seasoned professional or just getting started, **Corail Tombstone** delivers a clean, intuitive experience packed with everything you need — right out of the box. Join thousands of users who've already streamlined their Minecraft modding workflow. Download now and get started in minutes. 🚀

## 🚀 Features

| **Feature** | **Description** |
|:---|:---|
| **Death Chest** | Tombstone spawns at death location preserving all inventory items |
| **Soul System** | Collect souls to unlock tombstone enchantments and upgrades |
| **Grave GUI** | Open tombstone with a GUI to selectively retrieve items |
| **XP Recovery** | Optionally stores experience points lost on death for recovery |
